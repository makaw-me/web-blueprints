Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers on planning your itinerary, payments, schedule changes, and travel protection with Tripnexa.

Booking & Planning

Submit your preferred destinations, travel window, and group size through our Contact page. A dedicated Tripnexa advisor will prepare a personalized itinerary proposal within 24 hours—at no obligation—before you confirm any booking.

Each package includes vetted accommodations, airport or rail transfers, scheduled guided excursions, and daily breakfast where noted. International flights can be bundled into your quote or booked independently.

For peak seasons such as summer in the Mediterranean or autumn foliage in Japan, we recommend booking 4 to 6 months ahead. For off-peak getaways, 4 to 8 weeks is usually sufficient.

Payments, Changes & Protection

We accept major credit cards, bank transfers, and corporate cards. Most custom itineraries require a 25% deposit to lock in accommodations and guides, with the remaining balance due 45 days prior to departure.
