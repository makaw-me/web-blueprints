Terms of Service

Last updated: November 2026. Please review these terms carefully before confirming a booking with Tripnexa.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing the Tripnexa website or booking any travel itinerary, tour package, or related service through Tripnexa, you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service and our Privacy Policy.

2. Bookings & Payments

All itinerary quotes are subject to availability until confirmed with a deposit. Unless otherwise stated in your booking summary, a 25% deposit is due upon confirmation, with the remaining balance due 45 days prior to departure. Bookings made within 45 days of departure require full payment at confirmation.

3. Changes & Cancellations

Requests to modify or cancel a confirmed trip must be submitted in writing to support@tripnexa.com. Cancellations made 30 days or more before departure are eligible for a refund minus non-refundable airline tickets or supplier deposits. Cancellations within 30 days of departure are subject to partner hotel, airline, and tour operator cancellation schedules.

4. Passports, Visas & Entry Requirements

Travelers are responsible for holding a valid passport (typically valid for at least six months beyond the return date) and obtaining any required visas or health clearances. Tripnexa provides entry guidance as a courtesy, but final compliance rests with each traveler.

5. Travel Insurance & Liability

Tripnexa acts as an intermediary for independent airlines, hotels, transport providers, and local tour operators. While we carefully vet every partner, we are not liable for delays, weather disruptions, or acts beyond our reasonable control. We strongly recommend purchasing comprehensive travel insurance at the time of booking.

6. Contact Us

Questions regarding these Terms of Service may be sent to hello@tripnexa.com or by mail to Tripnexa, 450 Lexington Avenue, Suite 1400, New York, NY 10017.
