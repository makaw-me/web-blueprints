10 Hidden Coastal Gems to Visit This Year

Skip the crowded boardwalks and discover tranquil coves, cliffside fishing villages, and family-run seafood spots worth the detour.

Some of the world's most memorable coastlines sit just one ferry ride or coastal train stop away from the busiest tourist hubs.

Over the past year, our destination advisors have revisited classic summer routes across Southern Europe and Southeast Asia to find quieter harbors where local life still sets the rhythm of the day.

The secret to a restorative coastal trip isn't finding the most famous beach—it's waking up in a town where morning fishermen outnumber tour buses.

From the limestone coves of Menorca's northern shore to the terraced lemon groves above Praiano, smaller seaside towns reward travelers who linger for three or four nights instead of rushing through on a day trip.

Traveling during shoulder months—May, June, and late September—brings warm water, open family-owned tavernas, and gentle evening breezes without peak-season queues.

When planning your route, pair one well-connected hub airport with a scenic regional transfer. A two-hour ferry or coastal rail ride often unlocks dramatic cliffs, sheltered swimming bays, and boutique guesthouses at half the cost of flagship resorts.

Pack a light linen layer, book a sea-facing room with a balcony, and leave at least two afternoons completely unscheduled.

If you are ready to build a coastal itinerary tailored to your dates, our Tripnexa advisors can handle ferry timetables, private boat charters, and vetted boutique stays from start to finish.
