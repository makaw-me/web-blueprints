About Us

Welcome to Morsella

Founded on a simple love for honest cooking and unhurried conversation, Morsella pairs timeless Mediterranean and American comfort dishes with modern seasonal flair.

Every morning our bakers pull hearth bread from the oven, our stocks simmer slowly for hours, and our dining room prepares to welcome neighbors, travelers, and celebrations of every size.
