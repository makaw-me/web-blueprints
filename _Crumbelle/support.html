Customer Care

How Can We Help?

Order Changes

Need to update an inscription, portion size, or collection time? Contact us at least 48 hours before your scheduled bake so our chefs can adjust your order smoothly.

Delivery & Venue Setup

For wedding and corporate deliveries, our dispatch team coordinates directly with your venue manager. Same-day delivery updates are available by phone on +44 20 7946 0821.

Allergen Guidance

Full ingredient sheets are provided with every box. If a guest has a specific allergy, email hello@crumbelle.com before ordering for tailored advice.
