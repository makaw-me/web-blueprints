About Us

Welcome To Crumbelle

Small-batch patisserie crafted with organic British dairy, stone-ground flour, and single-origin chocolate.

Every Crumbelle creation begins before dawn in our Kensington kitchen. Whether you are planning an intimate birthday dinner or a multi-tiered wedding reception, our pastry chefs balance classic French technique with bold, seasonal flavours so every slice tastes as memorable as it looks.

Pure Ingredients

We bake exclusively with free-range eggs, cultured butter, and real fruit compotes—never artificial preservatives.

Award Winning

Recognised across London for bespoke wedding cake design, delicate sugarcraft, and dependable event delivery.
