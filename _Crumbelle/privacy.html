Privacy Policy

Home Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry, book a tasting, or subscribe to our newsletter, we collect the contact details you provide—such as your name, email address, phone number, event date, delivery address, and dietary notes.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information solely to prepare quotes, fulfil and deliver cake orders, coordinate with event venues, and—if you opt in—send seasonal menu updates. We never sell or rent your personal details to third parties.

3. Data Retention & Security

Order and enquiry records are retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil your order and meet UK accounting obligations. Access to customer records is restricted to authorised Crumbelle staff.

4. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and to unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time.

5. Contact Our Data Team

For any privacy-related requests, please email hello@crumbelle.com or write to Crumbelle, 42 Kensington High St, London W8 4PF.
