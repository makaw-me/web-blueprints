Home Terms & Conditions

Last updated: November 2026

1. Orders & Deposits

Signature celebration cakes require full payment at the time of booking. Bespoke and wedding orders are confirmed upon receipt of a 50% non-refundable booking deposit, with the remaining balance due 14 days prior to delivery or collection.

2. Changes & Cancellations

Minor adjustments to inscriptions, colours, or collection times may be requested up to 48 hours before your scheduled bake. Standard orders cancelled more than 5 days in advance are eligible for a full refund or store credit. Cancellations within 72 hours of the event date cannot be refunded as ingredients and kitchen time have already been committed.

3. Collection & Delivery Liability

Once a cake is collected from our Kensington bakery or signed for upon delivery, responsibility for safe transport, storage, and temperature control passes to the client. We recommend transporting cakes on a flat boot surface with air conditioning running.

4. Allergens & Dietary Notice

Our kitchen handles cereals containing gluten, dairy, eggs, soya, peanuts, and tree nuts. While we take every care when preparing vegan or gluten-free bakes, trace cross-contamination cannot be completely ruled out.

5. Design Variations

All Crumbelle cakes are handcrafted. Subtle variations in hand-piped buttercream, fresh seasonal florals, and natural food colouring are part of artisanal baking and may differ slightly from reference photographs.
