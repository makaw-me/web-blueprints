How Udaan Protects Your Information

Last updated: November 2026

1. Overview

Udaan (“we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data you share with us when you visit our website, make a donation, volunteer, or subscribe to our newsletter. We handle personal data in line with applicable Indian laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with Udaan, we may collect:

  • Contact details: Name, email address, phone number, and postal address provided through our contact, volunteer, or newsletter forms.
  • Donation & statutory details: Contribution amount, transaction reference, and PAN (where required to issue Section 80G tax-exemption receipts and file Form 10BD).
  • Technical data: Basic browser and device information needed to keep the website secure and accessible.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your information strictly to process donations, issue official 80G receipts, respond to your enquiries, coordinate volunteer placements, and send quarterly impact updates if you have opted in. We never sell, rent, or trade donor or subscriber lists to third parties.

4. Data Security & Retention

Payment transactions are processed through encrypted, PCI-DSS compliant Indian payment gateways; Udaan does not store your card numbers or UPI PINs. Statutory donation records are retained for the period mandated by the Income Tax Act, 1961.

5. Your Rights & Grievance Contact

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data (subject to statutory retention rules), or opt out of newsletters at any time by writing to our Grievance Officer at hello@udaanindia.org or calling +91 11 4102 8450.
