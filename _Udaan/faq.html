Common Questions About Udaan

Yes. Udaan is a registered non-profit trust in India, and all eligible contributions qualify for tax deduction under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961. Please share your PAN details when donating so we can issue your Form 10BE / 80G receipt.

Our active field programmes operate across six states: Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Bihar, Odisha, Uttar Pradesh (Bundelkhand), and Delhi NCR. Each hub works directly with local panchayats, schools, and primary health centres.

Over 85% of every contribution goes directly into field delivery—learning materials, teacher stipends, solar water filtration maintenance, and mobile clinic supplies. The remaining share covers independent audits, monitoring, and essential coordination.

Yes. You can support Udaan through one-time or monthly contributions via UPI, net banking, debit/credit cards, or direct NEFT/IMPS transfer. Write to hello@udaanindia.org if you need our trust bank details or want to modify a monthly pledge.

We welcome weekend classroom mentors, medical professionals, and field interns across our hubs. Reach out through our Contact page with your city, availability, and area of interest, and our volunteer coordinator will connect with you.

Yes. Udaan complies with Schedule VII CSR guidelines under the Companies Act, 2013, and holds valid CSR-1 registration. We provide milestone-based utilisation certificates, quarterly impact reports, and employee volunteering days.
