About Us

Grassroots Impact Across Rural And Urban India

True change begins when a child stays in school, a mother drinks safe water, and a village clinic has the medicines it needs.

Ananya Deshmukh, Founder & Managing Trustee

Founded in New Delhi in 2016, Udaan works alongside panchayats, local educators, and community health workers across six Indian states. Our programmes focus on measurable, long-term self-reliance rather than short-term relief.

Every Contribution Fuels Real Progress On The Ground

All donations to Udaan are eligible for tax deduction under Section 80G of the Income Tax Act, 1961. A ₹1,500 gift provides a child with school supplies and nutrition support for an entire term.
