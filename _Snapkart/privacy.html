Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information you provide during checkout or account registration, including your name, email address, phone number, billing/shipping address, and PIN code, solely to process and deliver your orders.

2. Payment Security

Snapkart does not store your full credit/debit card numbers or UPI PINs. All online payments are processed via PCI-DSS compliant Indian payment gateways using encrypted connections.

3. Cookies & Analytics

We use essential session cookies to keep your shopping cart and preferences intact. You can manage cookie preferences in your browser settings at any time.

4. Data Sharing & Retention

We share necessary delivery details only with our verified logistics partners to fulfil your shipment. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Grievance Officer

For privacy requests or data deletion queries, contact our Grievance Officer at support@snapkart.in or write to 42, MG Road, Indiranagar, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560038.
