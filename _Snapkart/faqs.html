Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about ordering, UPI & COD payments, shipping across India, and our 14-day return policy.

We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), RuPay, Visa, and Mastercard credit/debit cards, Net Banking across major Indian banks, and Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible PIN codes up to ₹25,000.
Orders above ₹999 qualify for free standard shipping across India. For orders under ₹999, a flat ₹99 delivery fee applies. Metro cities typically receive orders within 2–3 business days, while other locations take 4–6 business days.
Snapkart offers a 14-day easy return and exchange window from the date of delivery. Items must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging with tags intact. Refunds for prepaid orders are credited within 5–7 business days.
Yes, every price displayed in INR (₹) on Snapkart is inclusive of applicable Goods and Services Tax (GST). A GST tax invoice is included with your shipment.
