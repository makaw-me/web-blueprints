About Aurelia Hotel

A Legacy of Refined Comfort

Nestled along a quiet avenue just moments from Paris's most iconic avenues, Aurelia Hotel & Suites was conceived as an oasis of calm for the discerning global traveler. Every detail within our property celebrates the timeless elegance of French neoclassicism, seamlessly married to the warmth of modern boutique luxury.

Our suites feature hand-selected natural materials, custom acoustic engineering for serene sleep, and thoughtful amenities designed to restore both body and spirit. From our sunlit courtyard terrace to our Michelin-inspired culinary programs, our dedicated team is at your service 24 hours a day to craft an extraordinary Parisian journey.
