About Aurelia

Established as an intimate sanctuary in the heart of Paris, Aurelia Hotel & Suites harmonizes neoclassical architectural grace with bespoke modern hospitality. From handpicked Italian linens and spa-inspired baths to our tranquil botanical courtyard, every detail is tailored for restorative comfort. Whether you are traveling for a romantic escape or an executive retreat, our dedicated concierge team ensures an exceptional stay from arrival to departure.
