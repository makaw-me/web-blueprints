Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 — Your privacy matters to us.
This policy explains how Geniweb Solutions handles information collected through our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a project inquiry, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us directly, we may collect personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, and project requirements. We only collect information that you voluntarily provide.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you share to respond to inquiries, prepare project proposals, deliver requested services, and send occasional studio updates if you have subscribed to our newsletter. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies & Basic Analytics

Our website may use essential cookies or privacy-respecting analytics to understand overall traffic patterns and improve site performance. You can adjust your browser settings at any time to refuse or clear cookies.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your contact details from unauthorized access or disclosure. We retain inquiry and client records only as long as necessary to fulfill our services and meet legal obligations.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you at any time. To exercise these rights or ask questions about this Privacy Policy, please email hello@geniweb.io or reach out via our Contact page.
