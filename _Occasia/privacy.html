1. Commitment to Privacy

At Occasia ("we," "our," or "us"), protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our clients, event partners, delegates, and website visitors is an essential priority. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, handle, utilize, and protect personal and operational information gathered through our website and during event production engagements.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information strictly necessary to provide event management, scenic fabrication, and technical production services:

  • Contact Information: Name, professional title, corporate affiliation, email address, and phone number submitted through consultation forms.
  • Project Specifications: Event briefs, venue dimensions, tentative dates, expected delegate counts, and structural staging requirements.
  • Career Submissions: Resumes, portfolios, and employment history submitted for open production and technical positions.
  • Technical & Log Data: Anonymous browser telemetry, device type, and visit duration used to optimize site performance and accessibility.

3. How Information Is Utilized

Collected information is applied strictly for legitimate business operations:

  • Conducting site feasibility assessments and drafting custom production proposals.
  • Procuring statutory venue clearances, electrical safety approvals, and municipal permissions.
  • Coordinating accredited technical vendors, equipment logistics, and stage delivery schedules.
  • Communicating project milestones, revisions, and post-event operational reporting.

4. Client Confidentiality & NDAs

We regularly manage proprietary brand reveals, executive leadership meetings, and confidential corporate assemblies. Occasia strictly adheres to non-disclosure agreements (NDAs). We never disclose confidential presentations, corporate strategy materials, or unreleased product prototypes to unauthorized parties.

5. Event Photography & Media Documentation

Photographs and video footage of event stages, structural builds, and crowd atmospheres showcased in our gallery are documented in accordance with client approvals and contractual permissions. Clients wishing to exclude specific production elements from public marketing portfolios may request written exemption prior to project execution.

6. Data Retention & Security

We implement physical, electronic, and administrative safeguards to prevent unauthorized access or disclosure of client records. Project records and communications are retained only as long as necessary to fulfill operational, accounting, and legal requirements.

7. Inquiries & Data Rights
