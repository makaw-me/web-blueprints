These Terms of Service govern the engagement, contractual agreements, and execution of live event production, structural scenic fabrication, audiovisual engineering, brand activations, and event management provided by Occasia ("the Company") to clients, corporate sponsors, and authorized event representatives ("the Client").

2. Project Quotations & Work Orders

All scope modifications, on-site additions, or timeline changes requested by the Client must be confirmed in writing and may incur supplementary equipment and fabrication fees.

Custom scenic fabrication, printed backdrops, 3D architectural mockups, and technical stage plots require timely Client review and sign-off. Occasia is not liable for schedule delays caused by late receipt of client assets, high-resolution brand vectors, or overdue design approvals.

4. Safety Protocols & Venue Regulations

Safety is a non-negotiable cornerstone of Occasia productions. All structural trussing, certified rigging hoists, electrical distribution boxes, and stage decking strictly adhere to national safety codes and venue capacity limitations. Occasia safety directors retain final authority to postpone or adjust outdoor setups during hazardous weather or unmitigated safety risks.

5. Payment Schedules & Cancellation Terms

Standard production engagements operate on milestone billing:

  • Mobilization Advance: Payable upon contract execution to reserve gear, schedule technical crews, and procure raw fabrication materials.
  • Load-In Installment: Payable prior to equipment dispatch and venue load-in.
  • Balance Completion: Settled upon successful event delivery and post-production signoff.
  • Cancellations: In the event of client-directed cancellation, the Client remains responsible for non-recoverable fabrication costs, committed sub-rentals, and crew reservation expenses incurred up to the notice date.
