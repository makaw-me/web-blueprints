  2. Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Kryptera collects information you provide directly—such as your name, email address, and identity verification documents when required for regulated services—as well as technical metadata (IP address, browser type, and session logs) used to prevent fraud and protect your account.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information solely to operate and secure the Kryptera platform, process transactions, fulfill legal and regulatory compliance obligations, and respond to customer support inquiries. We never sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security & Retention

Personal records are encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard protocols. We retain account and transaction records only as long as necessary to provide the Services and satisfy statutory recordkeeping requirements.

4. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to mandatory financial compliance retention rules.

5. Contact Our Privacy Team

For privacy inquiries or data requests, email support@kryptera.io or write to Kryptera, 450 Lexington Ave, New York, NY.
