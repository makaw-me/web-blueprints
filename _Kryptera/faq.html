Frequently Asked Questions

You can open a Kryptera account in under five minutes using your email address and a strong passphrase. Once you enable two-factor authentication, your multi-currency wallet is ready to receive deposits.

Over 95% of client funds are stored in offline, multi-signature cold vaults. Active accounts are protected by hardware-backed encryption, biometric 2FA, and optional withdrawal address allowlists.

Kryptera supports Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), major USD-backed stablecoins, and our native KRYP utility token, with additional vetted Layer-1 and Layer-2 assets added each quarter.

Spot conversions carry a transparent flat fee starting at 0.15%, with discounts for KRYP token holders. Internal transfers between Kryptera wallets are free, and external withdrawals only pass through the live network fee.

Visit our Token Sale page, complete standard account verification, and select your preferred funding method. Purchased KRYP tokens are allocated directly to your Kryptera wallet upon settlement.

Basic wallet creation requires only email verification. To unlock fiat bank deposits, higher withdrawal limits, and token sale participation, we require a quick government-ID verification to comply with global regulations.

Our escrow service locks the agreed digital assets in an audited smart contract until both buyer and seller confirm that payment or deliverables have been fulfilled, eliminating counterparty risk.

Visit our Support & Help page for step-by-step troubleshooting guides, or reach our 24/7 support team directly through the Contact page or at support@kryptera.io.
