Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Vendrico collects information you provide directly when creating an account, configuring storefront integrations, or submitting a support request—such as your name, work email address, company details, and operational records.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to operate and maintain the Vendrico platform, process orders and carrier dispatches on your behalf, respond to support inquiries, and send product updates if you have opted in.

3. Data Sharing & Security

Vendrico does not sell your personal or business data. We share information only with trusted infrastructure and carrier partners required to deliver the service, under strict confidentiality and security obligations.

4. Data Retention & Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting our team through the Support Center. We retain operational records only as long as necessary to provide the service or meet legal obligations.
