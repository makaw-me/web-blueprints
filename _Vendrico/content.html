Platform Overview

Vendrico connects your storefronts, warehouses, and carrier networks into one dependable operational layer. Instead of reconciling spreadsheets across disconnected tools, your team gets a single, accurate source of truth for every SKU, order, and shipment.

Every order entering Vendrico passes through configurable validation and routing rules. Inventory counts reserve automatically at checkout, multi-location stock transfers stay synchronized in real time, and warehouse staff receive clear, scannable pick lists. Whether you fulfill fifty orders a day or fifty thousand, the workflow stays consistent and auditable.

Beyond dispatch, Vendrico gives finance, merchandising, and customer support teams the context they need without custom engineering. Support agents can inspect live carrier milestones and initiate exchanges in one click, while merchandising leads track sell-through velocity across channels. Automated alerts notify buyers before popular items hit safety-stock thresholds, helping you avoid costly stockouts during peak seasons.

Three pillars of the Vendrico platform:

1. Real-Time Inventory Synchronization: Maintain accurate stock counts across online storefronts, wholesale portals, and physical warehouses with instant reservation and adjustment logs.

2. Automated Carrier Routing: Match every order with the optimal fulfillment hub and shipping service based on weight, destination, and delivery promise.

3. Actionable Operational Reporting: Export reconciled fulfillment, return, and inventory valuation reports ready for your finance and leadership teams.
