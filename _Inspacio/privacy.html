Studio Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 | Inspacio Design Studio, LLC

1. Commitment to Client Confidentiality

Inspacio Design Studio, LLC ("Inspacio", "we", "us", or "our") deeply values the privacy of our clients, collaborators, and website visitors. Because our architectural and interior design practice involves sensitive residential blueprints, private property access credentials, and personal lifestyle requirements, we uphold the highest industry standards of data security and confidentiality.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect and process information in the following contexts:

  • Direct Inquiry Information: Full name, telephone number, email address, property location, target budget, and design preferences submitted via our consultation forms.
  • Architectural & Property Records: Existing property floor plans, survey deeds, co-op alteration requirements, utility schematics, and laser scan site measurements necessary to execute architectural designs.
  • Digital Usage Data: Standard server logs, browser type, referring pages, IP address, and cookie indicators collected to evaluate website performance.

3. Blueprint & Floor Plan Protection Guarantee

We recognize that floor plans and spatial security layouts of high-end private residences require strict discretion. Inspacio will never sell, lease, publicize, or transmit your private architectural drawings, structural layouts, or security alcove positions to unauthorized third parties. All CAD/BIM files shared with engineering consultants and licensed general contractors are transmitted through encrypted channels solely under non-disclosure agreements.

4. Photography & Portfolio Permissions

Prior to photographing any completed residential commission for publication in our portfolio, design journal, or press editorial, Inspacio secures an explicit, signed Photography Consent Agreement from the Client. Upon request, residential photography will be anonymized without disclosing street numbers, homeowner names, or exterior identifying markers.

5. Cookies & Analytics

Our website utilizes essential technical cookies to ensure smooth navigation and responsive rendering across devices. We may also utilize anonymous analytics services to understand which journal articles or portfolio galleries attract visitor engagement. You may adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies at any time without impacting core site accessibility.

6. Data Retention & Safeguards

We store project files, permit sets, and client correspondence on secure, encrypted private cloud repositories with multi-factor authentication. Inactive project files are archived in compliance with New York State professional architectural record retention requirements (typically ten years post-handover) to support future client renovations or municipal permit inquiries.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including rights under GDPR and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) regulations where applicable), you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to inaccurate records, or request the deletion of non-essential marketing information by contacting us.

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data rights, please contact:
