Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding our architectural approach, project timelines, cost estimation, and contractor management. If you require further clarification, our studio team is always available.

Our initial consultation is a comprehensive 90-minute design and feasibility review. We inspect your existing site or blueprints, discuss your spatial routines, outline regulatory constraints (such as DOB permits or co-op board guidelines), and review preliminary budget scopes. Following this session, we produce a structured Design Proposal detailing our recommended scope, timeline, and deliverables.

We maintain an open and transparent fee methodology. Architectural planning, schematic design, and construction documents are billed under a predictable fixed fee based on square footage and project complexity. Construction administration, artisan millwork procurement, and on-site supervision are billed either on an agreed percentage of construction cost or monthly retainer. All trade discounts on fixtures and furniture are passed directly to our clients without hidden markups.

Timelines vary based on scope and municipality approvals. Typically, Phase 1 (Concept, Space Planning, and 3D Modeling) spans 6 to 8 weeks. Phase 2 (Technical CAD Working Drawings, Co-op/HOA Submissions, and DOB Permitting) takes 8 to 12 weeks. Phase 3 (Demolition, Construction, and Millwork Installation) ranges from 5 to 9 months for full-floor urban lofts or brownstone restorations. We provide detailed Gantt milestone schedules prior to lease or construction commencement.

Yes. Construction administration is one of our studio's strongest assets. We conduct weekly on-site superintendent walkthroughs, review submittals and mockups, resolve unforeseen field discrepancies with structural trades, and approve contractor payment draw applications only after work meets our rigorous craftsmanship threshold.

Sustainability is fundamental to spatial longevity. We prioritize low-VOC and zero-formaldehyde cabinet cores, FSC-certified European and American hardwoods, natural mineral limewashes, locally quarried masonry, and energy-conserving architectural LED systems. The result is superior indoor air quality and materials that develop rich, organic character over decades.
