Studio Terms of Service

Effective Date: November 2026 | Inspacio Design Studio, LLC

1. Scope of Agreement

These Terms of Service govern all architectural design consultations, spatial planning, interior decorating, 3D visualization, millwork specifications, and construction administration provided by Inspacio Design Studio, LLC ("Inspacio", "we", "us", or "our") to clients ("Client" or "you"). Individual project specifics are governed by executed Project Letter Agreements (PLAs), which incorporate these Terms by reference.

2. Professional Services & Deliverables

Inspacio provides professional design services adhering to standard American Institute of Architects (AIA) guidelines. Deliverables may include conceptual sketches, schematic space plans, finish boards, millwork elevations, photorealistic renderings, and construction drawing sets. Unless explicitly contracted in writing, Inspacio does not act as the primary General Contractor, but serves as the Client's architectural advisor and construction administrator.

3. Client Responsibilities & Site Access

The Client agrees to provide timely access to the property for site surveys, laser measurements, and contractor walkthroughs. The Client is responsible for furnishing accurate property deeds, existing blueprints, co-op/condo alteration requirements, and disclosure of known hazardous materials (such as asbestos or lead) prior to the commencement of design schematics.

4. Fee Structures, Retainers & Disbursements

All projects commence upon receipt of an agreed initial design retainer. Invoices for milestone phases or hourly administration are issued monthly and payable within thirty (30) days of receipt. Reimbursable project expenses—including blueprint reproduction, permit filing fees, specialized structural engineering reviews, and sample courier deliveries—are billed at direct cost with prior Client authorization.

5. Intellectual Property & Instruments of Service

All drawings, specifications, computer-aided designs (CAD/BIM), 3D models, and design concepts prepared by Inspacio remain the intellectual property and "Instruments of Service" of Inspacio. The Client receives a non-exclusive license to use these documents solely for the construction and maintenance of the specific property designated in the agreement. Re-use on other projects without Inspacio's written consent is prohibited.

6. Third-Party Contractors & Fabricators

Contracts for actual physical construction, structural demolition, plumbing, electrical, and HVAC installation are entered into directly between the Client and licensed, insured General Contractors. While Inspacio reviews construction compliance against design drawings, the contractor retains sole legal responsibility for jobsite safety, means and methods, and building code execution.

7. Revisions & Scope Adjustments

Each design phase includes the number of revision rounds specified in the Project Agreement. Substantial alterations to approved plans—such as altering structural walls or changing primary room allocations after technical working drawings have commenced—will be documented through a written Change Order reflecting revised fees and schedule adjustments.

8. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, Inspacio's total cumulative liability arising from any claim related to design services shall not exceed the total professional architectural design fees actually received by Inspacio under the applicable Project Agreement. Neither party shall be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

9. Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

These Terms and any project agreements shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of New York, without regard to conflicts of law principles. Any dispute arising out of or relating to these Terms shall be settled through good-faith mediation prior to commencing legal proceedings in New York County.
