========================================================================
Inspacio - Contemporary Architecture & Interior Design Studio
Website Project
========================================================================

Project Overview:
Inspacio is a bespoke architecture and interior design studio dedicated to
crafting thoughtful, functional, and enduring residential and commercial
environments.

Website Architecture:
- index.html    : Studio homepage with hero showcases, services, portfolio, and reviews
- about.html    : Studio history, architectural philosophy, and leadership team
- service.html  : Comprehensive interior design, spatial planning, and styling services
- project.html  : Filterable portfolio of completed, active, and upcoming spaces
- blog.html     : Editorial design journal featuring articles on architecture and materials
- single.html   : In-depth design journal article with author bio and discussions
- contact.html  : Consultation booking and studio inquiry form
- faq.html      : Frequently asked questions on consultation, fees, timelines, and sourcing
- support.html  : Client support center, finish care guides, and warranty assistance
- terms.html    : Studio terms of service and architectural agreement conditions
- privacy.html  : Privacy policy and client blueprint confidentiality standards
