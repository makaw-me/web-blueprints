Wanderly Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

At Wanderly ("we," "our," or "us"), safeguarding your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information is fundamental to the experiences we craft. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal data we collect, how it is utilized to organize your journeys, and your rights concerning your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

When you consult with us, request an itinerary, or book a vacation package, we collect necessary information to fulfill your travel requirements, including:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Full name, passport number, date of birth, gender, postal address, email address, and phone numbers.
  • Travel Preferences & Itinerary Data: Flight selections, accommodation preferences, dietary restrictions, mobility assistance requirements, and destination requests.
  • Payment & Billing Data: Payment card details, billing address, and transaction history managed through certified, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.
  • Communications: Records of your consultations, inquiry forms, feedback surveys, and customer support messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal information strictly for legitimate travel management and business purposes:

  • To confirm bookings with partner airlines, boutique hotels, rail carriers, and licensed local tour operators.
  • To provide personalized travel itineraries, quotes, flight schedule updates, and emergency support.
  • To comply with international immigration, customs, security, and visa regulatory requirements.
  • To send you our newsletter, curated seasonal offers, and travel advisories with your express consent.

3. Sharing with Trusted Partners

To deliver seamless vacation experiences worldwide, Wanderly collaborates with verified third-party travel suppliers. We only disclose the minimum personal data required to secure your reservations (such as names and passport details for flight tickets or room reservations). We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Retention & Security

We maintain strict administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, accidental loss, alteration, or disclosure. Personal information is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill travel bookings, settle accounting obligations, or adhere to legal retention statutes.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Under applicable international privacy standards, you hold specific rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • The right to access, inspect, and receive a copy of your stored personal information.
  • The right to request immediate correction of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • The right to request data erasure where records are no longer required for legal or booking fulfillment purposes.
  • The right to opt out of marketing communications at any time via the unsubscribe link in our newsletters.

6. Contact Our Privacy Team

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please reach out directly to our Data Protection Officer:
