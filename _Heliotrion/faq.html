Everything You Need to Know About Clean Energy

Answers to common questions about solar sizing, permitting, battery backup, and long-term maintenance with Heliotrion.

Most residential installations are completed in 4 to 8 weeks from contract signing. Physical roof mounting and electrical work typically take 1 to 3 days, while the remaining time covers structural engineering, municipal permits, and utility interconnection approval. Commercial projects vary by capacity and typically span 8 to 16 weeks.

Standard grid-tied solar systems automatically shut off during a grid outage for utility worker safety. When you pair your array with a Heliotrion battery energy storage system and automatic transfer switch, your property seamlessly islands from the grid and continues powering essential circuits day and night.

Every Heliotrion installation includes a 25-year panel performance warranty, a 10- to 25-year inverter warranty depending on hardware selection, a 10-year battery storage warranty, and our 10-year workmanship and roof-penetration guarantee.
